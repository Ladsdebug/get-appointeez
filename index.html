You paid for the lead. Someone else got the job.

A customer asks for help online at night. The same request goes to you and a few other businesses. One of them texts back right away and offers a time. By the time you call back the next day, they've booked.

You pay for every click. When you run your own ads, the money leaks out after it.

Leak 1

A slow page

Someone taps your ad and lands on a page that loads slowly on their phone, or doesn't say what the ad said. They tap back and pick someone else.

Leak 2

A missed call

The phone rings while you're on a job. Nobody picks up, so they call the next number on the list.

Leak 3

A slow reply

A form comes in after hours and gets an answer the next afternoon.

When people need help, they rarely wait. They book whoever gets back to them first. The page and the first reply decide whether a click you paid for becomes a job.
