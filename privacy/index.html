Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Appoint Eez ("we", "us") runs getappointeez.com. This policy covers this website and our two services: (1) our service for local businesses, where we find one thing on a business's ads or website that may be costing it jobs, build a fix it can look at, and keep that fix live if the business chooses to; and (2) our lead response service for debt relief firms. It explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • When you book a call or contact us: your name, email address, phone number, business name and anything you choose to tell us. Bookings are handled through our scheduling provider (LeadConnector).
  • Public information about businesses: to find a finding worth sending, we look at information a business has made public, such as its website, its public ads (for example in the Google Ads Transparency Center or the Meta Ad Library), and its public business listing and reviews. We use it only to prepare our finding and the fix we build for that business.
  • When a business uses our service: if a business keeps our missed-call text back or lead alerts live, we process details about the people who call, text or fill in a form for that business, such as their name, phone number, email and message. We handle this on the business's behalf and only to provide the service to it.
  • Technical information: standard details such as browser type, device and IP address, collected by our hosting provider when you visit this site.

How we use your information

  • To reply to you, schedule and hold calls, and follow up by call, text or email as described when you gave us your details.
  • To build, show and run the service a business has asked for, including sending missed-call texts and new-lead alerts to that business.
  • To send a business one email about a finding on its public ads or website. You can reply to ask us not to email you again and we will stop.
  • To keep this site and our services working and secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

Pages we build for a business

A page we build for a business to look at is kept private: it is not linked from search engines and is not connected to that business's website, phone line, payment or customer systems unless the business chooses to keep it and asks us to connect it.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run this site and our services (for example hosting, scheduling, email and text messaging), and only as needed for them to do that work. Information about a business's customers is shared with that business. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Text messages and mobile numbers

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging consent and phone numbers will not be shared with any third parties. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. If a business stops using our service, we stop processing its customers' details for it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@getappointeez.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
